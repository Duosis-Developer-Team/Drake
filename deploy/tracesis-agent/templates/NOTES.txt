Tracesis agent installed for cluster "{{ .Values.cluster.name }}".

The agent enrols with its one-time token and starts reporting within a
minute. Watch it connect:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} logs deploy/drake-cluster-agent -f

Logs, traces and metrics are stored in this namespace and stay here.
Tracesis reads them through the agent's own outbound connection.

The cluster's page in Tracesis shows "connected" once the first heartbeat
arrives.
